Plan types change infrequently; a weekly scheduled check opens a reminder issue to verify them. To update, edit the features blocks in data/offers.js — values marked * were not stated directly by the source.

Rows are ranked by true 24-month cost: everything paid in months 1–24 plus the device balance still owed at month 24 — so financing that stretches past 24 months isn't artificially cheap. Totals are what you pay in months 1–24. Carrier financing usually runs 36 months, so "Owed at mo 24" is the device balance that remains — leaving a carrier before the term ends makes it due and forfeits remaining promo credits. Plan prices assume autopay; taxes and fees are extra unless the plan says otherwise or you add an estimate above. Manufacturer trade-in values use the maker's published ranges and depend on your device's condition.
